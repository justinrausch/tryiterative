PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated January 2025


This Privacy Notice for Finda ("we," "us," or "our"), describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:
  • Use our mobile application Finda
  • Upload images for processing through our AI service
Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at findaappcontact@gmail.com.

SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS

What personal information do we process? We do not collect or store any personal information from users. We only process images that you upload for AI analysis.

Do we process any sensitive personal information? No, we do not process any sensitive personal information.

Do we collect any information from third parties? No, we do not collect any information from third parties.

How do we process your information? We process your uploaded images solely to provide AI analysis results. Images are processed through our AI systems and are not stored permanently.

In what situations and with which parties do we share personal information? We do not share any personal information with third parties.

How do we keep your information safe? We implement appropriate security measures to protect uploaded images during processing.

What are your rights? Since we do not collect personal information, there are no personal data rights to exercise.

How do you exercise your rights? Contact us at findaappcontact@gmail.com with any questions.


1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Personal information you disclose to us
In Short: We do not collect any personal information from users.

Finda is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or process any personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other identifying information.

Image Processing
The only data we handle are images that you upload for AI analysis. These images are:
  • Processed through our AI systems including ChatGPT and our custom AI location tracking model
  • Not stored permanently on our servers
  • Not shared with third parties except for processing
  • Deleted after processing is complete

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

In Short: We process your uploaded images solely to provide AI analysis results.

We process your uploaded images for the following purposes:
  • To provide AI analysis services. We process your images through our AI systems to provide you with the requested analysis results.
  • To maintain service functionality. We process images to ensure our service operates correctly and provides accurate results.

3. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION?

In Short: We do not share any personal information with third parties.

Since we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data to share. Uploaded images are processed through our AI systems and are not retained.

4. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

In Short: We do not keep any personal information. Uploaded images are deleted after processing.

Uploaded images are processed and then immediately deleted from our servers. We do not maintain any database of user information or processed images.

5. HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?

In Short: We implement appropriate security measures to protect uploaded images during processing.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect uploaded images during processing. However, no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

6. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?

In Short: Since we do not collect personal information, there are no personal data rights to exercise.

As we do not collect, store, or process any personal information, there are no personal data rights to exercise. If you have any questions about our privacy practices, please contact us.

In Short: Yes, we will update this notice as necessary to stay compliant with relevant laws.

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Revised" date at the top of this Privacy Notice.

8. HOW CAN YOU CONTACT US ABOUT THIS NOTICE?

If you have questions or comments about this notice, you may email us at findaappcontact@gmail.com.

9. HOW CAN YOU REVIEW, UPDATE, OR DELETE THE DATA WE COLLECT FROM YOU?

Since we do not collect or store any personal data from users, there is no data to review, update, or delete. Uploaded images are automatically deleted after processing.
