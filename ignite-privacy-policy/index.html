Ignite Privacy Policy

The information processed depends on the features you use, permissions you grant, and your app version. Account and activity data supports your challenges and progress; optional features may also process photos, journal entries, messages, and health or fitness information. 1. Information we collect

Information you provide and create

  • Account and profile: name, email address, account identifiers, username, profile photo, and sign-in information supplied by your authentication provider.
  • Onboarding and progress: your answers about age, habits, goals, and routines; challenge selections; task completion; streaks; activity statistics; and preferences.
  • Content: journal entries, text produced through dictation, AI messages, photos submitted for task verification or progress tracking, and community posts, comments, likes, and friendship activity.
  • Support: information you send us when requesting help or exercising privacy rights.

Device permissions and potentially sensitive information

Health and fitness information, journal entries, messages, and photos may contain sensitive personal information. Do not include information about other people without appropriate permission, or information you do not want processed by the feature you are using.

  • Apple Health / HealthKit: with permission, Ignite reads step counts, walking and running distance, exercise minutes, and workouts to verify activity goals and display progress. We do not request permission to write HealthKit records. We do not provide raw HealthKit records to advertising providers. Activity completion and progress generated by these features may be saved with your challenge data.
  • Camera and photos: selected or captured images may be uploaded to Firebase Storage for verification, task history, progress, or community features. Photo verification also sends the selected image to Google's AI service as described in Section 5. The photo picker may check a photo's date to confirm it was taken on the relevant day.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: optional journal dictation uses Apple's speech-recognition services. Audio may be processed by Apple depending on the device and service configuration; the resulting text can become part of your journal.
  • Screen Time: with permission, Ignite uses Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity features to support selected app and website restrictions and activity reports. Settings and activity summaries may be stored on the device and shared with Ignite's extensions through an app group.
  • Notifications: permission allows the app to deliver reminders and service-related notifications. You can change this in iOS Settings.

Usage, device, and purchase information

The app and its service providers may process IP addresses, device and app identifiers, device model, operating-system and app versions, timestamps, feature interactions, diagnostics, onboarding events, and subscription events. Apple processes App Store payments. Ignite and its subscription services receive product, transaction, entitlement, and subscription-status information; Ignite does not receive your full payment-card number or security code from Apple.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts; personalize habits and challenges; verify tasks; save journals, photos, and progress; provide community and AI features; manage subscriptions; deliver reminders; respond to support and privacy requests; understand feature usage; troubleshoot and secure the Services; prevent misuse; and meet legal obligations. Marketing measurement and advertising attribution may involve the providers and choices described below.

3. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information based on consent, performance of our agreement with you, compliance with legal obligations, or legitimate interests such as operating, securing, and improving the Services, subject to your rights. Sensitive information is processed subject to applicable additional requirements, including consent where required.

In the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland, you may have rights to object to processing based on legitimate interests. In Canada, consent may be express or implied where permitted by law, and legal exceptions may apply. You may withdraw consent as explained in Section 10; withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

4. Sharing and service providers

We use providers to operate the Services. The information shared depends on the feature, permissions, and app version:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: authentication, account and app databases, uploaded photos, server functions, and app measurement. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Groq: generates Ignus chat responses from messages and app context. Groq Privacy Policy.
  • Google Vertex AI: processes photos and task descriptions for AI verification. Google Cloud Privacy Notice.
  • Mixpanel: receives usage events, identifiers, and event or user properties for product analytics. Mixpanel Privacy Policy.
  • Superwall: supports paywall presentation, subscription-related features, and paywall or purchase analytics. Superwall Privacy Policy.
  • Meta: supports advertising attribution and measurement through app, trial, subscription, and purchase events. Device identifiers and other event information may be processed subject to applicable permissions. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Apple: provides App Store payments, Sign in with Apple, and device services such as speech recognition. Apple Privacy Policy.

Profile information and content used in community or shared challenges, including posts, comments, photos, and completion activity, may be visible to other users. Some challenge activity may generate community posts. Consider this before participating or uploading content.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, investigate misuse, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of business assets, subject to applicable legal requirements.

5. Artificial intelligence features

Ignus chat: when you use Ignus, Ignite sends your messages, recent conversation, and relevant app context through our backend to Groq to generate responses, including daily greetings. Context may include goals, task descriptions, completion status, streaks, and challenge progress. Older app versions may also include your name, onboarding answers, and attribute scores.

Photo verification: Ignite sends the photo you select, the task description, and related task/category information through our backend to Google Vertex AI to assess task completion. AI results can be incorrect and are not medical advice.

These providers process the submitted information to return results. Provider-side retention and operational processing are governed by the applicable service terms, our service configuration, and provider policies. We do not promise that submitted data is never retained by a provider.

Our current AI integrations send requests to Groq and Google Vertex AI. A model authored by OpenAI but hosted by Groq does not, by itself, mean Ignite sends those requests to OpenAI.

Permission controls depend on your app version. Versions with AI-sharing controls ask before sharing with each provider and allow you to turn off future sharing in Settings → Privacy & Security. Turning off sharing does not recall information already sent or cancel processing already in progress. If those controls are unavailable, avoid opening or using the AI feature and contact us about withdrawing consent or deleting information. Keep the app updated to receive available privacy controls.

6. Sign-in and Apple services

Ignite supports Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In. We receive the identifiers and profile information those services provide, such as name and email address, to authenticate and manage your account. Apple may supply a private relay email address if you choose to hide your email. Provider settings and policies govern the information they share.

Our use of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide the Services, maintain your account and history, and fulfill the purposes described here. Retention can vary by data type and provider. Legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, and dispute-resolution requirements may require retaining some records longer.

You can initiate account deletion in the app's Settings or contact us to request deletion of your account and associated information, including uploaded content. If information remains after an in-app deletion request, contact hello@talkupapp.com so we can address it. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete server-side information or cancel an App Store subscription.

Deletion requests are subject to identity verification, applicable legal exceptions, and provider processing. Backup copies may remain until they are overwritten or expire. Information retained for a legal or security requirement is kept for that purpose rather than ordinary use. Manage or cancel subscriptions separately through your Apple account.

8. Security and international processing

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal information. No system, electronic transmission, or storage method can guarantee complete security.

We and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries where services operate. Those countries may have privacy laws different from your location. Applicable data-transfer requirements and safeguards apply to processing where required by law.

9. Children and minors

The Services are not directed to children under 18. Users must be at least 18 (or the equivalent age specified by applicable law), or have a parent or guardian who consents to their use where permitted. We do not knowingly solicit information from or market to children under 18. If you believe a child has provided information without appropriate consent, contact jayden@talkupapp.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

10. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access or obtain a copy of your information, correct it, request deletion, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent. You may also have rights concerning decisions made solely by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects, including requesting human review where applicable.

To withdraw consent or exercise rights, use available app or device settings, email us, or submit a privacy request. We will consider requests under applicable law. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing or processing supported by another lawful basis.

  • Change camera, microphone, speech recognition, photos, notifications, and tracking permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Manage Ignite's HealthKit access in Apple Health or iOS Settings, and Screen Time authorization through the available iOS controls.
  • Use AI-sharing controls where available in your app version, as described in Section 5.
  • Update account information or initiate account deletion in the app, or contact us for help.

You may complain to your local data-protection authority, including an EEA supervisory authority, the UK Information Commissioner's Office, or the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

11. Tracking preferences

Use Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission in iOS Settings to control tracking that requires Apple's authorization. Versions with the updated tracking controls gate Meta advertising event collection on that authorization. Restricting tracking does not necessarily disable operational processing or first-party product analytics.

Browser Do Not Track signals do not have a uniform implementation across all services. You can contact us to request applicable opt-outs, including opt-outs of targeted advertising, sale, or sharing as defined by relevant law. We will address recognized privacy preference signals where applicable law requires.

12. United States privacy rights

Depending on your state and the law's applicability, you may have rights to know and access information, receive details about its collection and disclosure, obtain a copy, correct inaccuracies, request deletion, withdraw consent, and opt out of targeted advertising, sale or sharing, or certain profiling. Some states provide rights to limit use of sensitive information or obtain categories or lists of recipients. You have the right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising applicable rights.

The categories processed may include identifiers and contact details; account and purchase information; internet or app activity; photos, audio or other content you provide; health and fitness information; and preferences or progress inferred from your activity. Sources, purposes, recipients, and retention are described in Sections 1–7. Advertising-related disclosures may fall within state definitions of “sharing” or targeted advertising even when no money is exchanged.

Submit requests using the contact details or request form below. An authorized agent may submit a request where permitted; we may ask for proof of authorization and information needed to verify identity. We use verification information for handling the request and protecting your account.

If we decline a request, you may appeal by emailing hello@talkupapp.com. We will respond as required by applicable law. You may also contact your state attorney general or other relevant regulator.
